Home Control

A private control panel for one household.

Home Control is a personal app that runs on the owner's own computer at home. It lets the owner control their own lights, shades, speakers, TVs, sprinklers and Google Nest devices from one page. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. See the privacy policy below.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

  • Who uses it: only the household that runs it. There are no accounts and no sign-ups.
  • What it accesses: with the owner's permission, it uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read and control the owner's own Nest devices, such as thermostat settings and camera live views.
  • Where data is kept: Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file only that computer's account can read. Device status is used to show the panel and is not kept.
  • Camera video: shown live only while the owner is watching. It is never recorded or saved.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google, which provides the devices.
  • Removing access: the owner can turn off access at any time in their Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services, or in the Google Home app.
  • Contact: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Home Control.
